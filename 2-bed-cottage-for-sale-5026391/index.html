Kennedy Estate Agents offer this truly unique opportunity to purchase a detached, self-build project in an unbeatable location. The property is set on a scenic, shorefront, private site extending to approximately 1.4 acres. This exceptionally rare offering benefits from complete tranquillity and isolation while at the same time being only 5 minutes’ drive away from the busy town of Midleton.

Location:

A bird watchers dream, coastal tranquillity, and utter privacy are phrases that come to mind when describing the location of this property.

On approach, the property is accessed via a rugged, track-road along the shorefront, disappearing in the spring high tide, which further enhances the off-grid nature of the location. Such a coastal setting would only be expected deep in the countryside, however this property is a short 5 minute spin into Midleton town.

Midleton benefits from a host of shops, cafes, restaurants, and everyday conveniences. Midleton’s Farmers Market which takes place every Saturday morning offers an opportunity to browse and purchase artisanal local products.

Families will benefit from a wide choice of highly regarded primary and secondary schools. There are a choice of supermarkets also nearby including Lidl, Aldi, Supervalu and Tesco.
